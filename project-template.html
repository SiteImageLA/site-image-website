Aged Care & Seniors Living

Anglicare Seniors Living, Castle Hill

Completed in March 2019, the $70m Lober Square redevelopment is nestled within Anglicare’s 120 acre flagship site at Castle Hill. Site Image commenced on the Lober Square Precinct in 2014 with the successful delivery of Stage 2, Lober House heritage building and gardens refurbishment, chapel, café, retirement independent living units, and all associated external works and infrastructure.

The feature public building is the Chapel and Lober Square, with its sculptural forms and dramatic landscape setting.
